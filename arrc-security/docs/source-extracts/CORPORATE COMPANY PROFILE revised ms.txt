1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
ARRC Security Solutions Private Limited is a premier, full-service, and government-licensed private security agency incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013. We specialize in offering intelligent, robust, and highly reliable security and asset protection services tailored for prestigious corporate houses, premium retail malls, large-scale educational institutions, and manufacturing units.
Headquartered in

5. COMPREHENSIVE SERVICE PORTFOLIO
A. Manned Guarding & Physical Security
Corporate Security Officers: Elite, well-uniformed guards specialized in corporate office etiquette, visitor management, and access protocols.
Retail & Luxury Mall Security: Specialized hospitality-trained guards adept at managing large crowds, baggage scanning, and handling multi-point entry/exit gates.
Educational Campus Security: Dedicated personnel ensuring a safe, ragging-free, and highly secure environment for students, faculty, and institutional assets.
B. Executive Protection & VVIP Security
Personal Security Officers (PSOs): Armed and unarmed PSOs with impeccable records to safeguard high-profile executives, VIPs, and celebrities.
Event Security Management: Comprehensive crowd control, bouncers, and rapid-response teams for corporate events, AGMs, exhibitions, and high-profile gatherings.
C. Electronic Surveillance & Technical Security
Smart CCTV Monitoring: Implementation of AI-driven IP surveillance cameras and continuous control room monitoring.
Biometric & Access Control: Integration of facial recognition, RFID, card-based entry, and visitor tracking systems.
D.Smart Security & Geofencing Technology
Real-Time GPS Tracking: Our security personnel are monitored using advanced Geofencing (GPS Virtual Boundary) technology, ensuring their exact live location is constantly tracked.
Automated Attendance: Officers are automatically clocked in only when they physically enter your property’s digital perimeter, completely eliminating manual errors or fake check-ins.
Instant Alert System: If a guard leaves their assigned post or enters a restricted zone, our 24/7 control room and the client receive an instant automated alert.
100% Proof of Presence: This technology provides clients with undeniable digital proof that guards are actively patrolling and present at their designated stations throughout their shift.
Transparent Reporting: We deliver automated daily GPS guard tour logs and patrol insights, giving you complete visibility and peace of mind over your site's safety.
